Our approach · trend-following and contrarian

Cambridge Private Wealth combines trend-following analysis with contrarian risk management. We monitor market momentum, breadth, credit conditions and economic signals to assess when to maintain or adjust growth exposure. We also recognise that exceptionally strong momentum can become overextended.

When momentum appears overdone, we aim to consider trimming growth exposure and rebuilding defensive reserves rather than automatically increasing equity allocations. Conversely, periods of excessive pessimism may present opportunities to add growth selectively.

Important: High momentum alone is not a sell signal. Any adjustment requires corroborating valuation, breadth, sentiment and risk evidence, adviser judgement and client suitability. The contrarian overlay is a proposed decision framework, not an automated trading rule or a historically validated strategy.

Watch / mild deterioration. In Q3 2026, 5.0% of outstanding US household debt was delinquent, up from 4.7% in Q2. Early arrears increased for auto loans and mortgages, while credit-card early and serious delinquency measures improved slightly.
